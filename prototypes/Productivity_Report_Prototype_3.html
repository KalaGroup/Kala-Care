How productivity is calculated

Overall productivity
Per-day = SRs closed ÷ days in period
Every service request is counted on its SR close date. A branch figure is the cumulative total of its engineers.
Week / month productivity
Window rate = SRs in that window ÷ days in that window
Weeks are 7-day windows from the start date; months are calendar months clipped to the selected period. A short window divides by its own day count — shown in each column header.
